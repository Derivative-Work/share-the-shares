[Intro] (spoken, the Founder, over solo electric piano)
Right now, somewhere, a job is changing.
Maybe yours.
We can't promise to fix that.
But we can promise a percentage.
[Verse 1] (the Founder, earnest)
We built a thing that thinks so fast
it's rewriting what we do,
and some of what it's changing
is the work that pays for you.
So we gathered here this morning
in a studio we own,
to give away a part of us
to a fund we keep at home.
[Pre-Chorus] (the Lawyer, fast spoken patter)
It's not a charity, per se,
it's an LLC, wholly owned,
flexible, adjustable,
the details will be shown...
(choir) In the coming months!
[Chorus] (everyone)
Share the shares, share the shares,
five in every hundred, we'll be there.
And if we grow by half again,
two more (at our discretion) then.
The more we grow, the more we give,
so help us grow, so we can give.
[Verse 2] (the Board, smooth)
Now you might ask who holds the purse,
who signs and who decides
which cause is worthy, which is not,
and who sits on which side.
Each milestone's measured from a line
we haven't drawn just yet,
but trust us, it's a generous line,
the best one you can get.
[Pre-Chorus 2] (the Lawyer)
Not for lobbying, not for campaigns,
not for politics, we swear...
(beat) through this fund.
(choir) Through this fund!
[Chorus] (everyone)
Share the shares, share the shares,
five in every hundred, we'll be there.
And if we grow by half again,
two more (at our discretion) then.
The more we grow, the more we give,
so help us grow, so we can give.
[Verse 3] (the Worker, plain, close-mic; the band drops to piano)
They call me a specific population;
there's a study on my case,
two hundred million spent to learn
what happens to my place.
I don't need a giant cardboard check
with the number "TBA."
I'd just like to know who's choosing,
and whether I get a say.
[Bridge] (the Founder, sincere, drums return slowly)
And it's real, it isn't nothing:
a fifth of us, all told,
set aside for something bigger
than the ones who bought and sold.
If a promise holds while holding's cheap,
then watch us when it's not.
(choir, building) When it's not, when it's not...
[Final Chorus] (key change up a step, full gospel choir)
Share the shares, share the shares,
five in every hundred, we'll be there.
And if we grow by half again,
two more (the Board: at our discretion!) then.
The more we grow (the Lawyer: baseline to be announced!),
the more we give (the Worker: do I get a say?),
so help us grow, so we can give.
[Outro] (choir, fading)
The more we grow (the more we give),
the more we grow (the more we give),
the more we grow...
(spoken, the Founder, over the fade) Phone lines open at the IPO.
